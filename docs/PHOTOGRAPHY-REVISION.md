# Photography revision

The first draft used vector drawings in the menu and stylised procedural meshes in the hero. This revision makes real photographs from the user-supplied menu screenshot the main visual presentation. The source screenshot is copied unchanged into assets/menu-reference.png. The website frames its food photographs with CSS; no food photograph has been generated, repainted or altered for this revision.

## What changed

- Photo-first hero with a photographic pizza and bowl view, pointer tilt and depth.
- Existing true WebGL 3D scene preserved behind the explicitly labelled Explore 3D control. Photo tilt is not a 360-degree model and is not presented as one.
- Menu drawings replaced by real category photographs.
- Dish dialog photograph and updated labels.
- Generated editorial image removed from the visible page.

## Accuracy and quality limits

The bowl photo is a category example from the menu highlights. The pizza photo is from the supplied pizza menu artwork. The sandwich photo is a grilled sandwich, not a verified photograph of either submarine. They are explicitly labelled category photos and are reused across related dishes. Exact dish photos should replace them when available.

The source screenshot is 2047 x 1279. The useful pizza region is roughly 460px wide, and the bowl/sandwich regions are about 230px wide. They are real photographs but cannot deliver the sharpness of original camera files at large sizes. Do not describe them as high-resolution production assets.

Public Clock To Fit menu/photo pages were found, but the candidate image downloads returned access errors (401/403). Those failed downloads are not used. The source is the user-supplied screenshot. Confirm publication rights and replace these crops with original, owner-approved files before public launch.

A fully rotatable photorealistic dish still requires a detailed textured 3D asset or multi-view capture. The existing optional procedural model remains stylised.
